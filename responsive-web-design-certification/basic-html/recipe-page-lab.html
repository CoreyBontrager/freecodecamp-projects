Easy Chicken and Rice Recipe

This is an easy recipe for chicken and rice that can be thrown in the oven in one pan and enjoyed.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs chicken thighs
  • 1 cup uncooked white rice
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup salsa

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Add rice, chicken broth, and salsa to a 9 by 13 baking pan. Stir elements togethor.
  3. Add chicken to the top of the mixed elements in the baking pan. Season with the garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, and salt.
  4. Cover the pan with foil.
  5. Bake for 45 minutes.
  6. After 45 minutes remove foil and bake for 10 to 15 more minutes.
  7. Let the dish sit for 5 minutes after baking and then it is ready to enjoy.
